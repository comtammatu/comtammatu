# Design System Document

## Theme Overview

This document outlines the core design decisions for our digital products.

## Color Palette

Our color palette is carefully selected to ensure brand consistency and accessibility.

- **Color Mode:** light
- **Primary Color:** #D35400 - The brand's most distinctive chromatic color, suitable for buttons, CTAs, and key interactive elements.
- **Secondary Color:** #2D6A4F - A supporting color for less prominent UI elements, chips, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary Color:** #2980B9 - An additional accent color for highlights, badges, or decorative elements.
- **Neutral Color:** #454745 - A neutral base color for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements.

## Typography

We use a consistent typographic scale to ensure readability and hierarchy.

- **Headline Font:** inter
- **Body Font:** inter
- **Label Font:** inter

## Shape and Form

The geometric properties of our UI elements contribute to the overall feel of the design.

- **Roundedness:** Maximum, pill-shaped. This provides a very soft and friendly aesthetic.

## Spacing

Spacing plays a crucial role in readability and visual organization.

- **Spacing:** Normal. This offers a balanced layout with adequate breathing room between elements.
